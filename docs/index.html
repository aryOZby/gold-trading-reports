Gold Reports

Gold Reports is a private tool run by its owner on their own server. Once a month it builds Excel reports of the owner's own gold trades, one workbook per signal group, and delivers them to the owner:

  • by email from the owner's Gmail account to the owner's own address, and
  • to a folder it creates in the owner's own Google Drive.

It is not offered to other users. Source code: github.com/aryOZby/gold-trading-reports.
